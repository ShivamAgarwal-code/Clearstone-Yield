# Commercial thesis — Clearstone Fusion

> **Last updated:** 2026-05-07
> **Owner:** founder
> **Status:** living document; revise after every customer conversation

This is the research backing three load-bearing claims in the Clearstone Fusion
go-to-market story. Each section is sized to be consumable on its own; sources
are inline and re-collected in the appendix.

1. **Stablehacks credibility is convertible to revenue, not just press** —
   through five named channels (AMINA, Solana Foundation, Tenity, Solstice,
   Fireblocks).
2. **Banks should buy, not build** — five years of bank-led DeFi build attempts
   produced a graveyard; the vendor-partnership pattern (Fireblocks, Anchorage,
   Chainalysis) is already the dominant one in adjacent crypto categories.
3. **The retail TAM is the already-KYC'd saver, not the crypto-native wallet** —
   ~$8T in US money-market funds vs. ~$300B stablecoin float; permissionless
   retail is *shrinking* in 2025 even as DeFi TVL hits records.

## 2 · Banks: buy, not build

Every bank with serious digital-asset ambition over the past five years has hit
the same wall: the in-house build either ships years late at 8-figure cost or
gets quietly shelved, while the institutions that *bought* (Fireblocks,
Anchorage, Chainalysis) are already in production with regulators comfortable.
Clearstone is selling banks the four years and the audit-loss tail they cannot
afford to repeat.

### Top 5 arguments, ranked

**1. The in-house build graveyard is well-populated — and the names a bank CIO
will recognize.**
NAB shut down its **AUDN stablecoin** in 2024 "due to lack of customer demand
and internal risk concerns" — the entire build team then *left NAB to launch a
competing project externally* (Ubiquity), taking the institutional knowledge
with them. **Wells Fargo's** 2019 internal "digital cash" pilot demonstrated
efficiency but never shipped to production. **Meta's Diem** cost ~$1B+ and was
sold for parts.
Sources: [Ledger Insights — NAB shutters AUDN](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/national-australia-bank-shutters-stablecoin-project/),
[PYMNTS — Mapping the Digital Dollar Graveyard](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2026/mapping-the-digital-dollar-graveyard-of-failed-stablecoin-pilots/).

**2. The vendor-partnership pattern is already the dominant playbook in
adjacent crypto categories — banks have voted with their procurement budgets.**
**Fireblocks**: $150M+ ARR, $12B valuation, **80+ banks** including BNY Mellon,
BNP Paribas, ABN Amro, HSBC. BNY Mellon was a *strategic investor* in
Fireblocks rather than building competing tech in-house.
**Chainalysis**: 100+ FI customers, $750M ARR.
**Anchorage**: now custodian for BlackRock's BUIDL, the largest tokenized
treasury fund.
Banks have already conceded "buy, don't build" for custody and compliance.
Lending/vault infra is the next domino.
Sources: [Fireblocks ARR](https://www.fireblocks.com/press/fireblocks-surpasses-100-million-arr-milestone-cementing-its-status-as-saas-centaur),
[DLNews — Fireblocks bank wins](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/deals/fireblocks-attempts-to-woo-big-banks-into-using-crypto/),
[Chainalysis Series F](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/series-f/),
[Anchorage — BlackRock partnership](https://www.anchorage.com/insights/anchorage-digital-becomes-newest-digital-asset-service-provider-for-blackrock).

**3. DeFi audit failure is a recurring P&L event — and audits don't fix it.**
DeFi protocols lost **$1.4–2.9B to exploits in 2024**, and **$3.1B in H1 2025
alone**. In March 2025 a single reentrancy bug drained $47M in 90 seconds from
a protocol *audited by three separate firms*, all of whom missed it.
Mid-complexity DeFi audits run $60–150K each, take weeks, and are point-in-time
snapshots. A bank stack from scratch needs 3–5 of those, plus a security team
that knows oracle manipulation, MEV, and curve math — none of which traditional
bank IT has on payroll.
Sources: [Halborn — Top 100 DeFi Hacks 2025](https://www.halborn.com/reports/top-100-defi-hacks-2025),
[Sherlock — Audit Pricing 2026](https://sherlock.xyz/post/smart-contract-audit-pricing-a-market-reference-for-2026).

**4. The CTOs are saying it on the record.**
*"As a CTO, I would love to build everything, but it is impossible as the
on-chain world moves too quickly"* — institutional CTO quoted in *Traders
Magazine*, Feb 2026.
McKinsey's framing: *"Web3 native companies have the technology but don't have
the trusted brand; incumbents have the trusted brand but don't have the
technology."* This is the explicit consensus narrative in the trade press a
buyer's committee reads.
Sources: [Traders Magazine](https://www.tradersmagazine.com/featured_articles/institutions-look-to-tech-to-bridge-traditional-digital-assets/),
[a16z crypto — Blockchains for TradFi](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/blockchains-banks-asset-managers-fintechs/).

**5. Even the "successful" build (JPMorgan Onyx → Kinexys) took 4+ years and a
rebrand.**
Kinexys does $2B/day — but JPM had unique scale to absorb that runway. A
regional bank or asset manager attempting the same path is buying an 8-figure
R&D bill and a multi-year regulatory negotiation with no competitive advantage
at the end. Onyx's longtime division head departed in 2024, signalling the
internal cost even at the world's largest bank.
Sources: [Disruption Banking — What Happened to Onyx](https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2025/02/04/what-happened-to-onyx-by-jp-morgan/),
[Ledger Insights — Kinexys rebrand](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/jp-morgan-rebrands-blockchain-unit-to-kinexys/).

### Strongest narrative arc (CIO / investor pitch)

> *"In every adjacent layer of the digital-asset stack — custody (Fireblocks,
> Anchorage, BitGo), compliance (Chainalysis, TRM), tokenization rails
> (Kinexys, Forge) — the banks that bought a vendor are in production with
> regulators comfortable, while the banks that tried to build are either still
> in pilot, just shut their projects down (NAB AUDN, Wells Fargo digital cash,
> Diem), or paid JPMorgan-scale R&D bills to land where a vendor would have
> put them in 12 months. DeFi lending is the next category to consolidate
> around a vendor stack — curve management, oracle integration, MEV-aware
> liquidations, KYC gating, audited reserve config — none of which are skills
> bank IT shops have on payroll. The bank that buys Clearstone gets to market
> in a quarter with a regulator-defensible stack; the bank that builds will
> spend two years rediscovering the same primitives we already shipped, and
> there is a $3B/year exploit graveyard documenting what happens when they
> skip a step."*

## 3 · Retail: KYC is not the friction

The retail crowd that actually moves the needle — hundreds of millions of
bank, brokerage, and CEX customers — has *already* completed KYC and is sitting
on $8T+ of yield-seeking cash. Crypto-native permissionless retail is a
rounding error of <20M monthly active wallets that's *shrinking* in 2025. The
real friction killing retail DeFi adoption is wallets, seed phrases, and gas —
not KYC, which now passes 94% of users in under 22 seconds.

### Top 5 data points, ranked

**1. The savings pool is 25–40× bigger than all of crypto combined — and it's
KYC'd cash.**
US money market fund AUM crossed **$8 trillion in December 2025** (Crane), with
retail MMFs at $3.07T growing 12.5% YoY; net inflows of $901B in 2025 chased
"relatively high short-term yields when compared with bank deposit accounts."
Total stablecoin float is **~$300B (Oct 2025)**. The TAM for KYC'd yield
seekers is ~25× the entire stablecoin market.
Sources: [Bloomberg / Crane — MMFs top $8T](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-02/money-market-assets-top-8-trillion-for-first-time-crane-says),
[ICI MMF stats](https://www.ici.org/research/stats/mmf),
[Arkham — Stablecoins reach $300B](https://info.arkm.com/research/how-stablecoins-reached-a-300-billion-market-cap-in-2025).

**2. Crypto-native permissionless retail is tiny *and contracting* in 2025.**
Q3 2025 daily active wallets across all dApps averaged **18.7M and plunged 22%
QoQ despite record $237B DeFi TVL** — i.e., protocols are growing on capital,
not on new humans. Web3 monthly active dApp users sit at just **5–10M**.
Compare: Coinbase 120M monthly platform users; Robinhood 27.4M funded customers
(Q1 2026). The KYC'd cohort is **15–25× larger than the permissionless one**.
Sources: [Yahoo Finance — Q3 DappRadar](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/defi-tvl-hits-record-237b-181957477.html),
[Robinhood Q1 2026](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2026-results),
[CoinLaw — Web3 wallet stats](https://coinlaw.io/web3-wallet-user-growth-statistics/).

**3. KYC is no longer the bottleneck — it's a solved 22-second step.**
Sumsub's 2025 Crypto Industry Report: average pass rate hit **94%**, full
onboarding completes in **under 22 seconds** (4.5s for non-document flows), and
**74% of crypto firms now choose verification accuracy over speed** — the
speed problem is gone. Onboarding churn remains above 70%, but it's
concentrated on **wallet setup, seed-phrase responsibility, and gas** — not on
identity checks.
Sources: [Sumsub 2025 Crypto Industry Report](https://sumsub.com/lp/crypto-report/),
[PR Newswire — Sumsub 74% accuracy over speed](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/no-growth-at-all-costs-74-of-crypto-providers-choose-verification-accuracy-over-speed--new-sumsub-report-302718434.html),
[The Block — Wallet UX & adoption](https://www.theblock.co/post/386514/user-experience-in-crypto-wallets-how-to-increase-adoption-and-engagement).

**4. The retail crowd is *already* KYC'd — adding it to a savings product is
zero net friction.**
**96% of US households are banked** (FDIC 2023; only 4.2% unbanked, an
all-time low). For households >$100k, **99% are banked**. Coinbase has
disclosed **108M verified users** historically and **120M monthly platform
users** in 2025; Robinhood has 27.4M funded customers. Layer in Revolut,
Kraken, Binance, PayPal, brokerages — every retail saver who matters has done
KYC at least 3–5 times.
Sources: [FDIC 2023 Survey](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2024/fdic-survey-finds-96-percent-us-households-were-banked-2023),
[Coinbase Transparency Report 2025](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/transparency-report-2025),
[Backlinko — Coinbase 2026](https://backlinko.com/coinbase-users).

**5. Proof: regulated/KYC'd crypto products are the ones actually growing in
2025.**
PayPal's **PYUSD tripled to $3.93B market cap in <90 days** (216% growth)
after launching a **3.7% yield product** and pushing onto Solana/Kamino —
where it's now the **#2 stablecoin on Kamino with $500M+ in deposits**.
Robinhood crypto notional volume hit **$82B in 2025**, with Q3 crypto revenue
**+300% YoY**. Both are 100% KYC'd, custodied retail experiences pulling
permissioned users toward DeFi-style yield.
Sources: [CoinCentral — PYUSD reaches $1B via Spark/Kamino](https://coincentral.com/paypals-pyusd-stablecoin-reaches-1-billion-market-cap-through-spark-partnership/),
[CNBC — PayPal 3.7% PYUSD yield](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/23/paypal-introduces-3point7percent-yield-on-stablecoin-balances-to-boost-payments-activity.html),
[Robinhood FY25 results](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results).

### Investor closing line

> *"The mass-market retail saver is already KYC'd five times over and sitting
> on **$8 trillion in money-market funds chasing yield**, while permissionless
> DeFi has shrinking 18M daily wallets and a wallet-onboarding industry that
> admits **70% of users churn at the seed phrase, not at ID verification**.
> Every regulated retail-crypto product is *growing* — PYUSD tripled to $3.9B
> in 90 days, Robinhood crypto revenue +300%, Coinbase at 120M monthly users.
> The next $1T of retail savings won't migrate to crypto by learning what a
> seed phrase is; it'll migrate when their bank, broker, or fintech offers a
> KYC'd savings product running on DeFi rails underneath. We're building that
> pipe."*
